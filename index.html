      Frequently asked questions

      What is the UTM Student Portal?

      A free, student-made page that puts 83 University of Toronto Mississauga links in one searchable place: ACORN, Quercus, the exam schedule, timetable builder, library, campus services and more. It also has a live term tracker, Mississauga weather, a UofT GPA calculator and Desmos calculators.

      How do I calculate my UofT GPA?

      UofT converts each course mark to grade points on a 4.0 scale (85–100% = 4.0, 80–84% = 3.7, 77–79% = 3.3, 73–76% = 3.0, and so on), then averages them weighted by credit value: 0.5 for half-year (H) courses and 1.0 for full-year (Y) courses. CR/NCR courses are left out. The UTM GPA calculator does this for you and also works out your cumulative GPA and the final-exam mark you need.

      When do classes and exams happen in Fall 2026 and Winter 2027?

      Fall 2026 classes run Sep 8 to Dec 8, 2026, with exams Dec 10–Dec 22. Winter 2027 classes run Jan 11 to Apr 12, 2027, with exams Apr 14–Apr 29. Always confirm deadlines on the UTM Registrar's Important Dates page.

      Where can I find my UTM final exam schedule?

      Use the UTM Exam Schedule link (sign in with your UTORid). It lists the date, time and room for each of your final exams.

      Can I install the portal on my phone?

      Yes. Open the site in your phone's browser and choose “Add to Home Screen” (iPhone: Share → Add to Home Screen; Android: the Install prompt or ⋮ → Install app). It opens like an app and the main pages still load without a connection.

      Is this an official University of Toronto website?

      No. It is an independent project built by a UTM student and is not affiliated with or endorsed by the University of Toronto. Every link goes to the official source, and you can suggest a new link on GitHub.
