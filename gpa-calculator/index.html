    University of Toronto grading scale

    Each course mark becomes grade points, which are averaged by credit weight. This is the scale UofT uses for every campus, including UTM.

    PercentageLetterGrade pointsMeaning
    90–100A+4.0Excellent
    85–89A4.0
    80–84A-3.7
    77–79B+3.3Good
    73–76B3.0

    Sessional GPA covers one term; cumulative GPA (CGPA) covers every course you've taken. This calculator is unofficial: your official GPA is on ACORN. UofT also has its own GPA calculator.
